About Poochkoo!

Poochkoo started in 2020 with one belief: every pet deserves care as good as home. What began as a small boarding home in Bangalore has grown into a trusted network across Bangalore, Hosur and Pune — built entirely on word-of-mouth from happy pet parents.

Today we offer boarding, grooming, swimming and pet cab services — all run by trained, animal-loving caretakers, with a vet on call and daily updates so you're never far from your pet.
